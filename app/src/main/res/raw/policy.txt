 1. Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi (PM-KISAN)
• Income support scheme for land-holding farmers
• ₹6,000/year via three DBT instalments
• Helps improve farmer cash flow and stability

 2. Pradhan Mantri Kisan MaanDhan Yojana (PM-KMY)
• Pension scheme for small & marginal farmers
• Monthly pension ₹3,000 after age 60
• Farmers pay small monthly subscription

️ 3. Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana (PMFBY)
• Affordable crop insurance scheme
• Covers losses from natural calamities
• Large coverage, millions of farmer applications insured

 4. Modified Interest Subvention Scheme (MISS)
• Concessional short-term agri loans at 7% interest
• Timely repayment subvention lowers effective rate to 4%
• Includes post-harvest loans via Kisan Credit Card

 5. Agriculture Infrastructure Fund (AIF)
• Financing facility for post-harvest infrastructure
• Supports cold storage, warehouses, etc.
• Promotes private investment & modern logistics

 6. Formation & Promotion of New Farmer Producer Organizations (FPOs)
• Scheme to form 10,000 FPOs
• Financial and equity support per FPO
• Helps farmers access markets & better prices

 7. National Beekeeping & Honey Mission (NBHM)
• Promotes scientific beekeeping
• Boosts honey production and quality labs
• Targets growth of honey FPOs and bee colonies

 8. Namo Drone Didi Scheme
• Provides drones to women SHGs for services
• Helps with spraying fertilizers, pesticides
• Up to 80% financial assistance for drones
